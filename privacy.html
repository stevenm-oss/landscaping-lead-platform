Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

When you ask for a quote, we collect your name, phone number, email (if you give it), ZIP code, and the details you share about your project. We also record the date and time, your IP address and browser type, and whether you agreed to receive texts, so we have a record of your choice.

How we use it

We use your information to match you with one independent local contractor and to send your request to them so they can contact you and give you a quote. We may also contact you about your request.

Who we share it with

We share your project details and contact information only with the contractor we match you with, and with service providers that help us run the site (hosting, email and text delivery). We do not sell your information.

Text messages

If you check the box on our form, you agree to receive calls and texts about your project from {{ site_name }} and the contractor we match you with. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text opt-in consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep lead records as long as needed to serve you, bill contractors, and keep proof of consent, and then delete them.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, contact us at the email address on our contact page or reply to any message from us.
